思玥隐私政策 / Siyue Privacy Policy

版本 / Version：2026-09-25 · 生效日期 / Effective：2026-09-25

处理者与联系 / Maintainer and contact

账号服务由思玥项目维护者提供。关于个人资料、更正、注销或其他隐私请求，请联系 aizhangqiu@gmail.com。

The Siyue project maintainer provides the account service. Use the email above for personal-data questions, corrections, deletion or other privacy requests.

账号服务所需资料 / Account data

我们处理邮箱、密码的不可逆校验值、可选显示名称、账号与安装标识、平台类型、协议同意版本、会话与安全事件。验证码有时限，用于验证邮箱控制权。网络地址用于限流和防止滥用。密码、验证码及令牌不应出现在应用日志中；客户端使用系统安全存储保存恢复登录所需凭据。

We process your email, a one-way password verifier, optional display name, account and installation identifiers, platform, accepted policy versions, sessions and security events. Time-limited codes verify control of your email. Network addresses support rate limiting and abuse prevention. Passwords, codes and tokens are excluded from application logs; clients use system-protected storage for session recovery credentials.

邮件与基础设施 / Email and hosting

验证码及必要安全通知通过 Resend 发送，需向其提供收件邮箱及邮件内容。思玥暂时与 QiuGe Helper 共用发信服务，邮件显示名称为思玥，账号、队列和数据库访问权限分别配置。邮件处理可能涉及境外服务；收件邮箱服务商也会处理邮件。仅当你发起验证或相关安全动作时使用该发送通道。账号服务部署在阿里云服务器；当前思玥数据库与注销账本在同一 PostgreSQL 集群中分别保存，并非不同基础设施。

Resend sends verification codes and necessary security notices, receiving the destination address and message content. Siyue temporarily shares the mail service with QiuGe Helper while configuring its own accounts, queues and database permissions. Email processing may involve services outside your country, and your mailbox provider also processes the message. The sending channel serves verification and related security actions you initiate. The account service is hosted on Alibaba Cloud. The Siyue database and deletion ledger are separate databases in the same PostgreSQL cluster, not separate infrastructure.

保留与注销 / Retention and deletion

账号资料用于提供账号服务，直至注销或相应用途结束。验证码十分钟失效，过期挑战记录在后续清理中移除。注销受理后登录凭据失效，后台按回执进度清理账号资料。最小化的防恢复标记继续保留以防旧备份恢复已注销账号；其中不含邮箱、姓名或正文内容。注销回执及相关安全记录的当前窗口为三十天。共同作品、其他家庭成员的资料及未解决的管理责任须单独处理，不承诺随单个账号立即清除。备份或上游邮件系统中的副本不会被描述为已同步物理擦除。

Account data supports the service until deletion or the end of its purpose. Codes expire after ten minutes and expired challenges are removed by subsequent cleanup. Once deletion is accepted, credentials are invalidated and background cleanup reports progress through the receipt. Minimal anti-revival markers remain to prevent an old backup from reviving a deleted account; they exclude email, name and content. The current receipt and related security-record window is thirty days. Shared works, other family members' data and unresolved management responsibilities require separate handling. We do not represent backup or upstream email copies as immediately physically erased.

控制与边界 / Your control and boundaries

账号设置提供退出、身份管理和注销入口。个人与家庭权限按账号隔离；创建账号不自动授权整库上传、公开作品或向 AI 服务发送资料。儿童资料按监护授权处理。本政策只说明当前账号链路；新增数据用途前应在相应功能明确说明。你可以联系维护者请求访问、更正或处理相关资料。

Account settings provide sign-out, identity management and deletion. Account and family permissions are isolated. Registration does not authorize whole-database upload, public sharing or sending data to AI services. Child data depends on guardian authorization. This policy describes the current account flow; new purposes require disclosure in the relevant feature. Contact the maintainer to request access, correction or handling of your data.
