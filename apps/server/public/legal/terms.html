创建及使用账号 / Your account

邮箱注册用于创建成人账号。请仅使用你有权使用的邮箱，完成邮箱验证并设置密码，保护好登录凭据。儿童使用须由监护人在家庭功能中按授权流程安排，不应自行注册成人账号。请勿冒用身份、侵犯他人权利、攻击服务或绕过权限控制。

Email registration creates an adult account. Use an email address you control, verify it, choose a password and keep credentials secure. Children must use the guardian-authorized family flow rather than independently register an adult account. Do not impersonate others, infringe their rights, attack the service or bypass access controls.

数据与选择 / Data and choices

注册前请阅读隐私政策。创建账号不代表授权自动上传本机全部资料；具体同步、共享及 AI 服务的数据使用应以相应功能的说明和你的操作为准。应用仍在开发中，请保留重要资料的独立副本。

Read the Privacy Policy before registering. Creating an account does not authorize automatic upload of all local data. Sync, sharing and AI data use depend on the relevant feature disclosures and your actions. Keep independent copies of important information while the app is under development.

退出和注销 / Sign-out and deletion

你可以退出登录，并在账号设置中申请注销。注销需再次验证身份；管理多个家庭时逐个选择转交或结束管理。转交须由已加入的有效成人明确接受责任；唯一管理者结束管理且仍有其他成员时，家庭冻结待指定运维复核，儿童设备授权立即撤销。共同作品或仍须核对的记录不会冒充已删除，处置进度以注销回执为准。注销后原账号无法继续登录；本机资料的保留或删除以应用中明确提供的选项为准。

You can sign out or request account deletion in account settings after reauthentication. For each managed family, choose an accepted transfer to an existing active adult or end management. A family with remaining members is frozen when its sole manager leaves; child device access is revoked and designated operations staff review the family. Shared works or records requiring review are not reported as deleted until resolved. The deletion receipt shows progress. The old account cannot sign in after acceptance; local data handling follows the choices shown in the app.
